# Product voice

Write like an experienced AP teacher explaining the tool to a colleague in the hallway: plain, short, specific, a little dry. Never like a startup, a chatbot or a press release.

## Rules

1. **Say the thing.** "Post grades to PowerSchool," not "Seamlessly sync your insights."
2. **Use teacher words.** Score, essay, period 3, rubric, timed write, DBQ, roster, gradebook. Not submission, artifact, learner, content, workspace, journey.
3. **Buttons start with a verb** and say exactly what happens: "Print packets," "Upload photos," "Approve 24 scores." Never "Submit," "Continue" or "Get started" when a specific verb exists.
4. **Short sentences.** Under 15 words in the UI. One idea per sentence.
5. **Numbers over adjectives.** "6 essays need review," not "Some essays need your attention."
6. **Errors say what happened and what to do.** "This photo is too blurry to read. Retake it in better light." Never "Oops!" or "Something went wrong" alone.
7. **Empty states tell the teacher the next step.** "No essays yet. Print a packet or upload photos to start."
8. **Sentence case** for every heading, button and label.
9. **AI is never the subject.** Say "Scores are ready," not "Our AI has analyzed your essays." Mention AI only where a teacher needs to know it, such as a confidence marker or the approval step.
10. **No hype, no apologies, no cheerleading.**

## Banned words and patterns

seamless, seamlessly, empower, unlock, elevate, revolutionize, transform, supercharge, game-changer, cutting-edge, next-level, harness, leverage, robust, delve, journey, magic, effortless, AI-powered, intelligent insights, at your fingertips, take it to the next level, we're excited, oops, uh-oh, awesome, amazing.

Also banned:
- Exclamation marks.
- Em dashes. Use a period, comma or colon.
- "Simply," "just" or "easily" in instructions.
- Rhetorical questions in headings ("Ready to save time?").
- Three-item rhythm lists written for sound ("fast, fair and free").
- Emoji.

## Examples

| Bad | Good |
| --- | --- |
| Unlock AI-powered grading insights! | See which rubric points your class missed. |
| Oops! Something went wrong. | Upload failed: page 3 is not a JPG, PNG or PDF. |
| Get started on your grading journey | Create your first class |
| Our intelligent engine has analyzed 30 submissions | 30 essays scored. 6 need review. |
| Seamlessly sync with your SIS | Post grades to PowerSchool |

## Check before finishing

Search the files you changed for every banned word above, plus "!" and "—", and rewrite any hits.
